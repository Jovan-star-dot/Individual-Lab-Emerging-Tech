## Criteria and course basis

The framework follows Andres Fortino, *Mastering the Craft of Emerging Technology Adoption*, Chapter 6, “The Diffusion of Emerging Technologies,” pp. 77–90. It applies Rogers’ social diffusion process; the knowledge, persuasion, decision, implementation, and confirmation stages; the five characteristics of relative advantage, compatibility, complexity, trialability, and observability; and the approximate adopter categories of innovators, early adopters, early majority, late majority, and laggards. The text cautions that an S-curve or Bass estimate depends on adequate adoption data and a defensible population denominator.

Operational rules added for this assignment require the agent to separate Agentic AI generally, the specific application, and industry/comparable-peer diffusion; state each Rogers category and characteristic as supported, example-only, or unclassifiable; report source dates, populations, geographies, technologies, applications, and measures; and distinguish announcements, pilots, production use, and continued use. These evidence-handling rules are proposed operating criteria, not instructor-defined numerical stage thresholds.

## Dated evidence and diffusion findings

- **U.S. Census Bureau, May 26, 2026; BTOS measurement ending May 3, 2026:** around 14% of U.S. Retail Trade businesses reported using AI and around 17% expected use in the next six months. The survey measures AI across any business function, not Agentic AI or e-commerce customer-service resolution. Source: https://www.census.gov/library/stories/2026/05/ai-use-businesses.html
- **NRF Center for Digital Risk & Innovation, December 17, 2025; survey fielded summer 2025:** 56 AI leaders at U.S.-based NRF member retailers; 57% cited cost and 57% model accuracy/reliability as top broad-AI concerns, while 55% cited workforce expertise gaps. This selected, self-reported sample is not a representative estimate and does not measure Agentic AI service-agent use. Source: https://nrf.com/research/retail-ai-trends-2025
- **Walmart, May 29, 2025:** the company reports that its Customer Support Assistant agents route and resolve issues and are increasingly acting autonomously. No deployment denominator, service geography, independent evaluation, or continued-use rate is reported. Source: https://corporate.walmart.com/news/2025/05/29/inside-walmarts-strategy-for-building-an-agentic-future
- **Salesforce customer story about Crocs, September 10, 2026:** reports a customer-facing multi-agent shopping/service implementation that includes order tracking and returns; three agents built in five months and a voice Help Agent built/launched in seven weeks. Vendor-supported case evidence without an independent adoption rate, user denominator, deployment geography, or continued-use measure. It reports reliance on order-management APIs, partner/FDE expertise, a data foundation, and MCP; this is one deployment, not evidence of sector-wide complementary-asset or standards uptake. Source: https://www.salesforce.com/customer-stories/crocs/building-ai-agents/
